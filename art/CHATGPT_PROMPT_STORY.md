ОБЩИЙ СТИЛЬ (для каждой картинки)
Hand-painted 2D fantasy illustration for a turn-based tactics game, in the spirit of classic Heroes of Might and Magic III but painterly. Visible brush strokes, soft gouache-like shading, crisp dark ink outlines of varying thickness, slightly desaturated palette: ash grey, warm parchment, ember orange, deep ink blue-black, with accents of violet rift light. Gentle top-left key light, warm rim light. The world is a vast archive of memories that is being erased: dust, ash, torn pages, floating ink. Readable silhouette first, details second.
Scenes of the drowned vaults (marked "water") shift the palette towards deep teal, sea green and wet black ink, with pale pearl highlights — same brushwork, outlines and lighting.

ГЕРОЙ
The Last Archivist: an ageless keeper of memories with long grey hair, hooded in deep ink-blue robes embroidered with faint golden script, calm wise eyes, a pale scar of ink across one cheek, always holding a glowing open codex. Seen mostly from behind or in three-quarter view, small against vast spaces — the world is the main subject.

КОМПОЗИЦИЯ (важно для игры)
- Формат — горизонтальный 1536x1024; в игре картинка растягивается на весь экран и затемняется.
- Поверх центра экрана идёт текст сценки: ЦЕНТР и НИЖНЯЯ ПОЛОВИНА — спокойные, тёмные, без мелких деталей и ярких пятен. Главный объект — в верхней трети или сбоку.

K4 · scene_chapter_4 · Глава IV «Последняя страница». An immense blank white page hovering in a dark void, the edges of countless surrounding pages scorched and curling where they touch its emptiness; the tiny Archivist stands far below, facing it.

СЦЕНКИ

K5 · scene_prologue · Пролог. The Great Archive as a whole world: cities shaped like stacked books, rivers of flowing ink, towers of shelves under a dusk sky torn by thin violet rifts; the Archivist stands on a high ledge in the foreground, holding the glowing codex, looking out over everything that is left.

K6 · scene_rift_1 · «Разлом закрыт». The armoured colossus Rift Warden collapsing into ash; the violet wound in the air closing like burnt skin; through the last gap of the rift — a glimpse of dark water far below, a door leading down.

K8 · scene_rift_3 · «Шов на ране». The Archivist touches a closed rift in the air and finds an old seam on it — stitches of tarnished golden thread, the trace of someone who tried to sew it shut long before him.

K9 · scene_rift_4 · «Последний Разлом». The Rift Warden kneels and lowers his long void-blade before the Archivist without a fight; ash drifts down slowly; the last rift behind them is almost closed. Calm, solemn.

The defeated Abyss Lord sinks, and the water around becomes clear as glass: on the bottom lie thousands of books stacked carefully like sleeping children; in the water fades the gentle face of an old human archivist — the man the Abyss Lord once was. Peaceful, sad, forgiving.

The Archivist stands in the round hall of four arches among the four masters of the schools — an ash archivist-warrior with a burning book, a tide priestess with a water serpent staff, a brass engineer-cleric with a gear halo, a masked weaver with floating masks — each turned to him, offering their answer. Warm light.
