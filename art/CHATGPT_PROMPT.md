ОБЩИЙ СТИЛЬ (для каждой картинки)
Hand-painted 2D fantasy illustration for a turn-based tactics game, in the spirit of classic Heroes of Might and Magic III but painterly. Visible brush strokes, soft gouache-like shading, crisp dark ink outlines of varying thickness, slightly desaturated palette: ash grey, warm parchment, ember orange, deep ink blue-black, with accents of violet rift light. Gentle top-left key light, warm rim light. The world is a vast archive of memories that is being erased: dust, ash, torn pages, floating ink. Readable silhouette first, details second.

ПРАВИЛА КАТЕГОРИЙ
- ОТРЯД (коды U): single creature, full body, three-quarter side view facing RIGHT, whole figure inside the image with a small margin, no ground, no cast shadow, no scenery, transparent background (PNG with alpha); if transparency is impossible — a plain flat light-grey background. Square 1024x1024. Must read clearly when shrunk to 150 px tall: bold silhouette, clear head, clear weapon or claws.
- КАРТА (коды C): vignette scene, landscape 1536x1024, subject in the centre third, soft background fading to warm parchment at the edges, like a remembered memory.
- ПОРТРЕТ (коды P): head and shoulders, three-quarter view facing right, centred, square 1024x1024, simple dark parchment background with a soft vignette, face readable at 64 px.
- ФОН (коды B): wide environment, landscape 1536x1024, no characters; keep the central area calm and low-contrast because game elements will be drawn on top.
- ИНТЕРФЕЙС (коды I): flat front view, no perspective, centred on a plain flat light-grey background, square 1024x1024, even uniform edges so it can be stretched as a 9-slice; ornament only at the corners.
